# Dedalus

> Dedalus Group is a European healthcare and diagnostics software provider headquartered in Florence,
> Italy. It supplies electronic patient records, clinical information systems, laboratory and
> diagnostic imaging software to hospitals, laboratories and care networks in roughly 40 countries.
> Dedalus markets HL7 FHIR-based interoperability through DC4H (Digital Connect 4 Health) and Open
> Health Connect, but publishes no public developer portal, API reference, machine-readable contract,
> or sandbox. Integration access runs through a sales and customer-support motion.

Generated by API Evangelist (apievangelist.com) from the public dedalus.com surface on 2026-09-02.
Dedalus does not serve its own /llms.txt — https://www.dedalus.com/llms.txt returns HTTP 301 to the
corporate homepage.

## Developer status

- No public developer portal. developer.dedalus.com, developers.dedalus.com, api.dedalus.com and
  docs.dedalus.com do not resolve (NXDOMAIN, checked 2026-09-02).
- No OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL SDL, AsyncAPI, WSDL or .proto published on any Dedalus host.
- No FHIR CapabilityStatement or openEHR CDR endpoint published.
- No MCP server, no A2A agent card, no llms.txt, no /.well-known documents. Every /.well-known/*
  path on www.dedalus.com returns HTTP 301 to the homepage (WordPress catch-all).
- No first-party SDK in npm, PyPI or any other registry. Note: the npm packages `dedalus`,
  `dedalus-labs` and `dedalus-mcp` belong to Dedalus Labs (dedaluslabs.ai), a different company;
  the PyPI package `dedalus` is the Dedalus Project PDE solver. None are Dedalus Group.
- No published pricing, plans or rate limits. Enterprise sales only.

## Products (marketing pages, no technical reference)

- [The Dedalus Platform](https://www.dedalus.com/global/en/our-offer/products/thededalus-platform/): Company-wide platform positioning, "API economy" model, semantic interoperability on open standards.
- [DC4H — Digital Connect 4 Health](https://www.dedalus.com/global/en/our-offer/products/dc4h/): Open, standards-based data platform built on an HL7 FHIR longitudinal patient record; six pillars (integrate, ingest, index, insight, inform, intervene); cloud, hybrid and on-premise deployment. Names an "FHIR-based API management component" for third-party applications but publishes no endpoint or contract.
- [Healthcare Platform Services](https://www.dedalus.com/global/en/our-offer/your-domain-of-interest/healthcare-platform-services/): Services wrapper around the platform.
- ORBIS / ORBIS U — electronic patient record suite (Germany, Austria, Switzerland, France, Benelux). Lorenzo — EPR in the UK. X1.V1 — clinical suite in Italy.

## Trust, security and compliance

- [Security](https://www.dedalus.com/global/en/security/): Describes periodic penetration tests and security checks and GDPR-framed data privacy. Names no certification (no ISO 27001, SOC 2 or IEC 62443 claim on the page), no PSIRT, no coordinated vulnerability disclosure policy and no bug bounty.
- [Regulatory & Compliance](https://www.dedalus.com/global/en/about-us/regulatory-compliance/): States that Dedalus products are medical devices subject to safety regulation; links the Italian MOG 231 organisational model. Does not name the specific regulation.
- [Privacy](https://www.dedalus.com/global/en/privacy/)
- [Terms of Use](https://www.dedalus.com/global/en/website-terms-of-use/)
- Domain security (probed 2026-09-02): www.dedalus.com serves TLS 1.3 with HSTS (max-age 63072000). dedalus.com publishes SPF and a DMARC record with p=quarantine. No DNSSEC, no CAA records.
